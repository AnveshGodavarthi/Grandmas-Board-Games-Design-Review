Approved

Open Edge Landing + Remote Touch Echo

A viewer-relative online roll: cowries land in open space beyond the active edge; remote viewers see a brief touch echo rather than another player's hand or a dedicated mat, followed by a light-stamped result that leaves the HUD quiet.

01

Waiting

Riya's name and home state turn orange; the board remains quiet and fully visible.

02

Remote touch echo

Riya's view shows the local release into the open area beyond her edge; remote views show only a brief edge-origin touch echo before the same landing.

03

Settled result

The four scoring cowries receive restrained sequential warm rim light while an unboxed cream-gold numeral forms from light on the open surface for roughly 1–1.5 seconds.

04

Choose pawn

The result number disappears before pawn selection; only Riya's playable pawn receives the restrained magical lift and glow, and observers cannot act.

05

Reconnect

Reconnect preserves the settled shells and legal-pawn state without a result number while a familiar Wi-Fi-off icon sits beside the affected player's name.

06

Reduced motion

For reduced motion, the same static, unboxed numeral appears instantly beside the settled shells with no rim-light sequence, ripple, trails, or replayed flourish.

Approved interaction

  • The cowries land in an open, unframed area just beyond the active player's edge; no dedicated mat or tray is shown.
  • Remote viewers never see another player's hand or anatomy.
  • A transient light ripple defines touchdown during the roll and disappears after the cowries settle.
  • The shared settled result is authoritative; animation only communicates it.
  • At settlement, the four scoring cowries illuminate and the result forms as light on the surface without a card, chip, badge, medallion, or container.
  • The numeral fades before choose-pawn and reconnect states; reduced motion presents it immediately and without animation.
  • Only legal pawns glow, and the emphasis lasts until the owning player commits a move.
  • Colour, object behavior, the brief light-stamped numeral, and familiar connection icon replace ordinary state sentences while screen readers receive the full state and result.
  • The textile board, sculptural pieces, painted environment, and clean HUD stay materially distinct.
